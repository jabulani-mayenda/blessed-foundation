PROGRAMMES

Our Work

We focus on practical work that helps people and communities build stronger futures.

PROGRAMME 01

Education

Supporting children and young people through access to learning opportunities, school materials, and educational mentorship.

  • • What we do: Provide exercise books, school uniforms, and learning packages to vulnerable primary students.
  • • Who it serves: Children in rural primary schools where resource constraints lead to high dropout rates.
  • • Where we operate: Primary schools in Blantyre Rural and Chikwawa districts.

PROGRAMME 02

Community Development

Partnering with local leadership on clean water borehole restoration, sanitation, and community infrastructure.

  • • What we do: Repair community hand-pumps and support local water committees.
  • • Who it serves: Rural households currently walking long distances for water.
  • • Where we operate: Partner villages across Blantyre District.

PROGRAMME 03

Youth Empowerment

Equipping young Malawians with practical vocational skills, micro-entrepreneurship guidance, and leadership mentorship.

  • • What we do: Conduct practical workshops in tailoring, carpentry, and small business management.
  • • Who it serves: Out-of-school youth seeking productive livelihoods.

Family & Community Support

Providing targeted food security support, home visits, and nutritional guidance for elderly-headed households and orphans.
