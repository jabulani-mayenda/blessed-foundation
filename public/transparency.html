ACCOUNTABILITY

Transparency

Blessed Foundation believes that honesty and openness are non-negotiable. We are accountable to the communities we serve, to our supporters, and to the people of Malawi.

Our Commitment

We operate with full integrity. Every programme is designed, delivered, and evaluated alongside local community leaders and village development committees. We believe communities know what they need — our job is to listen and support.

Our Work

All four of our core programme areas — education, community development, youth empowerment, and family support — are guided by community-assessed priorities. We work through existing local structures, not around them.

Our Finances

Blessed Foundation maintains complete financial records and is committed to regular public reporting. As annual audited financial summaries are finalised, they will be published on this page. In the meantime, any donor may request a financial summary directly by emailing us.

Governance & Oversight

Blessed Foundation is led by a local board of trustees made up of experienced Malawian professionals in education, community health, and social policy. All major programme decisions require board endorsement. Leadership changes are communicated publicly.

Policies & Safeguarding

We operate under a formal child protection policy, a whistle-blower protection policy, and an equal opportunity framework. All staff and volunteers working directly with beneficiaries undergo background checks and formal orientation.

Annual Reports

Annual programme summaries and financial reports will be published here as they are prepared. We encourage partners and donors to ask questions and hold us accountable.
